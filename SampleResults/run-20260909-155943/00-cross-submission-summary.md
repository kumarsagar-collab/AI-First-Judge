* The proposals use bounded scope, CAF, IP, and AI-efficiency savings as optimization levers.
* Team C claims $60,000 of savings and a $3,000 fee; Team D claims $75,000 of savings and a $4,500 fee.
* Neither submission provides sufficient evidence to treat those figures as validated commercial terms.

### Cost and risk-transfer traps

* Excluding a risk reserve is not automatically an optimization. It requires review of who carries delivery and value risk.
* Claimed savings must be checked for overlap, causality, rate-card support, and offsetting operational costs.
* An incorrect ECIF basis changes the scale-out calculation and must be corrected before external use.

* Privacy, data minimization, role-based access, auditability, fairness testing, drift monitoring, incident handling, and override procedures are not evidenced.
* Human review is mandatory before using either proposal externally.

### Takeaways

1. Preserve the bounded CFO outcome and the explicit DSO measurement focus.
2. Reconcile every baseline and value figure against the authoritative scenario before presentation.
3. Correct Team D's ECIF basis from $3.5M to the authoritative $3M.
4. Build a causal measurement plan that separates intervention impact from seasonality and other working-capital actions.
5. Substantiate all CAF, IP, AI-efficiency, and delivery-cost assumptions.
6. Define security, privacy, fairness, monitoring, audit, and human-escalation controls for the prioritization model.
